ABOUT / WORKING METHOD

Understand first.
Change carefully.

I am a software engineer with professional experience across C#/.NET backends and Angular/TypeScript frontends. I care about the behaviour behind a feature, the rules it needs to enforce, and the evidence that shows whether a change worked.

01 / METHOD

How I approach a problem

I try to reproduce a problem before changing code. Then I follow the relevant request, validation or data path, check what I know against what I am assuming, and use a focused test or comparison to narrow the cause.

The first explanation is a hypothesis, not a conclusion. A useful fix should address the observed behaviour and leave enough context for another person to understand the decision.

Working rule

Keep the observation, the diagnosis and the verification separate. They answer different questions.
