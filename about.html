ABOUT / HOW I WORK

I like problems where the behaviour can be checked.

I’m early in my software engineering career. Most of my professional experience so far is in application development, validation, testing, defect investigation, and the workflow around a change.

FIELD NOTES01 / PRACTICE

First understand the behaviour you expect.

When something fails, I try to reproduce it before changing code. I look at the relevant path, compare examples, and write down what I know versus what I am guessing. That does not always give me the answer quickly, but it makes the next step clearer.

On application work, I have handled backend changes, DTOs, request validation, Angular form behaviour and localisation. I have also worked with automated tests, pull requests, pipeline failures and temporary environments. I am learning to follow a change beyond the point where it works on my machine.

A confirmed hypothesis is only useful if the fix changes the behaviour that was reported. If the evidence is incomplete, I want that to be visible too.
