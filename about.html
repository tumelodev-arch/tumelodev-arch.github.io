ABOUT / TECHNICAL PROFILE

Full-stack application engineer, growing towards cloud-native delivery.

I’m a Graduate Software Engineer at Sage, building my capability across backend and frontend application work, testing, validation, and delivery. My next growth areas are cloud deployment, observability, service reliability, and responsible AI-assisted engineering.

HOW I WORK

Understand the behavior.
Prove the change.

I want to be the engineer who can take a problem from uncertain report to verified change: understand the requirement, trace the behavior, make a focused implementation, test the important cases, and communicate what the evidence shows.

  1. 01
    Investigate

    Reproduce the issue, inspect evidence, and check assumptions.

  2. 02
    Implement

    Keep changes deliberate and validation explicit.

  3. 03
    Test

    Cover expected behavior, negative paths, and likely regression risks.

  4. 04
    Verify & communicate

    Confirm what worked and be honest about what remains unresolved.
