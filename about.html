ABOUT / HOW I WORK

Understand the behaviour. Then change it.

I'm a junior software engineer interested in backend application development, cloud platforms, security-minded engineering and production reliability.

FIELD NOTES01 / PRACTICE

First understand the behaviour you expect.

When something fails, I try to reproduce it before changing code. I look at the relevant path, compare examples, and write down what I know versus what I am guessing. That does not always give me the answer quickly, but it makes the next step clearer.

My professional work includes .NET backend changes, CQRS-based applications, business-rule validation, production defect investigation, automated testing, security reviews and delivery workflows. I have also worked with AWS services and distributed-system troubleshooting exercises.

A confirmed hypothesis is only useful if the fix changes the behaviour that was reported. If the evidence is incomplete, I want that to be visible too.
